Control your Intellivent bathroom ventilation fan directly from Homey over Bluetooth.

Turn the fan on or off, set a fixed speed between 800 and 2400 RPM, or let it run itself using its built-in humidity, light and air quality (VOC) sensors. Trigger a one-tap Boost for quick, high-speed ventilation, use Airing or Timer mode for scheduled ventilation, or Pause the fan temporarily.

The app reads live sensor data - fan speed, temperature and humidity - so you always know what your ventilation is doing, and can build Homey Flows around it: get notified when the humidity or the fan's mode changes, or trigger the fan's speed, mode and detection settings from your own automations.

Supported devices:
- Intellivent Sky

Requires a Homey Pro with Bluetooth support. During pairing, put the fan into Bluetooth pairing mode as described in the in-app pairing instructions.
