Fresh Intellivent Sky already senses humidity, light and air quality on its own. With Homey it becomes part of the rest of your home: its temperature, humidity and fan speed sit next to your other devices, and your Flows can decide when the bathroom needs airing, like a boost when the shower starts or a quiet pause at night.

The fan connects directly to Homey Pro over Bluetooth, with no extra hub or cloud account. Put the fan in pairing mode when you add it, and Homey guides you through the rest.
